My career started as a programmer when I was, I think, 9 years old. Then I got into WordPress development, later into TypeScript & Golang development, which led me to getting a job at a Czechian-based cloud startup (zerops.io).

I pivoted to tea, coffee, and matcha for consumers and businesses.
Started [Gray Cup](https://graycup.org). You can buy gray cup products at [graycup.in](https://graycup.in) and for bulk you can head to [b2b.graycup.in](https://b2b.graycup.in).

Point to be noted: considering I was pretty much into designing as well, even though I have deuteranopia color blindness (got diagnosed in 6th grade).

My parents started consuming tea leaves in 2014, which introduced me to a very untouched industry (especially in India), and considering this is a very normalized industry specifically in the West and UK (no wonder they colonized Darjeeling).


The pivot happens whenever you're low or stagnant in life.

Mine happened when I was down because of no validation for my work and designs.


Personally, I believe that we should focus on the inclusion of software in boring businesses, as it leads to smoother and seamless business processes, which also leads to a better EBITDA.


No wonder we should use MedusaJS over Shopify (here’s my article on why you should prefer open-source software over proprietary software).


I still code, as it's the biggest leverage in this age, and a lot of businesses still don't understand the value of it. I try to use open source as much as possible.


I also have some interest in evolutionary biology of humans, or even how our body works, especially when we run or eat or digest.


I grew up in a very health-oriented family, which led me to busting lots of myths about how food propaganda spreads when it comes to eating healthy, and how the narrative is changed at mass scale.


I support originality, sustainability, and self-study over grabbing a certificate from a so-called institution which tells you if you're worth it.


My hobbies revolve mainly around design, programming, walking on green grass, sunbathing (not in June).


Also, when it comes to clothes, I prefer cotton over plastic clothes, and I make sure that I'm not feeding the junk pile as much as I can. Cotton is so good for the skin, and I think we should educate more people about it.
